# Farming and terrain tests

Run the `FarmingTerrain` category in Unity's Test Runner under **EditMode**, outside a game session. Tests cover crop definitions, saved crop state, daily farming updates, plant visuals, farmland, and map terrain.

The fixtures create temporary definitions, catalogs, managers, and tilemaps. They do not use authored scenes or edit Resources assets. Private serialized configuration and location providers are set through reflection so tests do not start the scene-loading pipeline.

Coverage includes cumulative growth thresholds; seed-stage requirements; sowing, duplicate prevention, and location isolation; daily growth in unloaded locations; missing/disabled plant definitions; callbacks that mutate world state; save serialization without asset references; plowing/watering/drying/unplowing; terrain metadata and farmland precedence; sparse cache rebuilds; translated/scaled grid centers; map bounds; inclusive Euclidean range; and restoring farmland records.

Regression fixes covered by these tests:

- Plowing initializes farmland even when the supplied farmable tile has no runtime farmland state yet.
- Watering unplowed tiles does not paint irrigation or save it.
- Unplowing removes irrigation from runtime state, the overlay, and the save record.
- Farmland restoration skips nonfarmable terrain and does not display irrigation without plowing.
- Sowing events for other locations do not spawn on the active map.
- Cached tile centers honor grid transforms and cell size.
- Daily growth snapshots the world enumeration before publishing callbacks that can mutate it; newly sown plants wait until the next daily update.

Current gameplay rules and limits observed during review:

- On `NewDayEvent`, healthy crops grow only if their saved plot is plowed and irrigated and the new date's season is in `PlantData.Growing Seasons`. Planting is allowed in any season. Outside the selected seasons, crops stay planted and pause growth; watering resets dry days and unwatered crops can still wilt. New definitions default to all four seasons; an empty selection never grows. `daysPassedSincePlanting` counts growth days rather than elapsed calendar days.
- Dry days increase saved `consecutiveDaysWithoutWater`; a watered day resets it. At the crop definition's `Days Without Water Until Wilt` threshold (default five, minimum one), saved `condition` becomes `PlantCondition.Wilted`. Wilted plants never grow or revive through watering. Saved `daysSinceWilted` counts further daily updates; at `Days Wilted Until Removal` (default five, minimum one), the crop is removed, including in unloaded locations. The day it first wilts does not count towards this delay.
- A wilted crop can be cleared by normal interaction regardless of harvest method or inventory capacity, without any yield or scythe. `Wilted Feedback Prefab` configures a standalone `MMF_Player` played at the crop position after manual clearing. Its independent root survives crop removal and is destroyed when playback completes. Automatic expiry does not play clearing feedback. Clearing and expiry publish `PlantRemovedEvent` after removing saved state; farmland stays unchanged.
- Mature plants retain their final stage while watered growth days keep increasing; they can still wilt when left dry.
- `PlantManager` owns the entire daily update: consume the previous day's irrigation, advance growth/wilting, dry every saved plot (including unloaded locations and plots without crops), then publish events. A single owner ensures listener order cannot dry a plot before its crop reads irrigation. Repeated daily events still process another daily update.
- `MapManager` listens for `FarmlandDriedEvent` to clear loaded irrigation overlays and runtime farmland. It does not separately handle `NewDayEvent`. Watering performed by an event callback for the new day is preserved.
- `FarmablePlant` listens for `PlantConditionChangedEvent` and restores saved wilt state when loading. Crop definitions can supply an optional `Wilted Representation` sprite/prefab; without it, the current growth visual receives a brown tint.
- No automatic rain watering is wired here.
- `Harvest Method` selects Picking or Scythe. Configure a `Harvest Item` and positive `Min Harvest Quantity` and `Max Harvest Quantity` (maximum at least the minimum and less than int.MaxValue). A simple inclusive random roll chooses the yield once per harvest attempt and supplies both delivery and the harvest event; a missing yield item is a configuration error. A crop must be healthy and at a stage marked `Is Harvestable`. Harvesting is allowed outside its growth seasons. Picking adds the complete yield to inventory; insufficient capacity leaves the crop and inventory unchanged. Scythe harvesting creates a `WorldItem` drop at the crop's world position, even with full inventory, for the existing pickup/magnet system. A configured drop is prepared before committing the crop change and enabled afterwards. Ground drops currently follow `WorldItem`'s runtime-only lifecycle; they are not restored by save/load. Saved crop/inventory changes finish before notifications. Farmland remains plowed and retains its current irrigation.
- `Regrowth Stage Index` is -1 for a crop removed after harvest, or an earlier, non-harvestable stage to return to. The saved growth-day counter resets to that stage's threshold, so additional watered, in-season growth days are needed before another harvest. Regrowth does not reset the existing dry-day history.
- `FarmablePlant` creates a stable root picking interactable. Stage representations can be full prefabs with collisions and behaviours; the outgoing stage is disabled immediately, and the incoming prefab is placed under the plant before enabling. FarmablePlant.Bind takes the saved PlantState before the root is enabled; appearance is derived from that state. FarmablePlant owns presentation and event subscriptions, while PlantHarvestInteractable owns picking/clearing requests and interaction feedback. PlantManager owns the saved-state and inventory transactions. Views and interactables use its singleton; EditMode tests establish the game-state and plant singleton lifecycle through the shared GameTestFixture and install isolated catalogs there. Do not add another harvest interactable or shared saved UUID to stage prefabs.
- `ScytheAction` uses the existing tile tool pipeline. The authored example in `Assets/Interaction/Tools/Scythe` includes a tool definition, one-tile-forward effect, placeholder icon, and action prefab registered on `TestCharacter`. Lentils use scythe harvesting, spring/summer growth, and a 1-3 yield of `LentilsProduce`; their wilt-clearing feedback is in `Assets/Plants/Feedbacks`. The standalone clearing prefab autoplays on Start and uses the bundled `MMTimedDestruction` component for cleanup; keep its authored lifetime longer than the complete effect (currently two seconds for the short sound). The produce icon reuses seed art and both scythe/clearing feedback reuse the hoe sound as placeholders. These items are discovered automatically by the item catalog; they are not added to existing saved inventories.
- World drops only fade towards a player when the entire stack fits in inventory. If capacity becomes insufficient after fading starts, a failed pickup restores visibility and leaves the drop intact. Ground-drop persistence is deferred (see the TODO in `TryHarvestWithScythe`).
- PlantData uses `ValidatedScriptableObject<PlantDataValidator>`; its configuration rules live in the separate `PlantDataValidator`. Configuration must be valid and must not change during gameplay. Shared rules run in OnValidate/Inspector feedback, the project-wide Tools > Validation command, catalog discovery, and a pre-build check. Invalid settings are reported without correction; runtime harvest checks cover gameplay availability only. See `Assets/Core/Common/VALIDATION.md`. Tests cover malformed definitions independently of gameplay, unreferenced assets and sub-assets, and build rejection.

Validation runs in the connected Unity 6000.6.3f1 Editor through Pipeline. All 137 tests in the `FarmingTerrain` category pass, including seasonal pause/resume and wilting, timed wilt removal, manual clearing and feedback lifetime, picking/scythe harvest checks, inventory capacity and callback safety, saved regrowth/wilt age and yield, full prefab replacement/activation ordering, ground-drop initialization, and failed pickup visibility.

Crop condition is separate from growth stage: `PlantCondition.Healthy = 0` is the default; `Wilted = 1` is terminal. Add future conditions with stable explicit enum values and define their gameplay rules. Condition-change events capture both previous and new conditions after the daily state update commits.
